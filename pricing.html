Billing & resource disclosures

How is a seat defined in Decapod Cloud?
A billable seat is one human contributor assignment to one subscribed repository. Coding agents, subagents, concurrent CLI sessions, git worktrees, and enrolled hardware keys do not consume extra seats.
How do monthly and annual billing differ?
Monthly billing lists at $50 per seat per month. Annual billing lists at $552 per seat per year billed upfront (an 8% term concession off $600), stacking with repository and distinct-human volume discounts up to 20% combined standard cap.
Can a subscription cover multiple repositories with different seat counts?
Yes. After signing in with GitHub, you verify each repository under your organization and set exact per-repository seat counts—whether equal or uneven—before the server produces the authoritative Stripe quote.
Will Decapod Cloud charge me automatically if usage exceeds an included limit?
No. Paid overages are disabled by default ($0 automatic overage exposure). Usage above configured operating limits is throttled or blocked, and Admin threshold alerts notify your team early without authorizing billing charges.
