GOOD TO KNOW

Clear terms.
Real control.

Everything that matters when your team grows.

What is a contributor–repository assignment?

One human assigned to one repository is one paid assignment. The same human assigned to two repositories uses two assignments. Contributor-volume savings count distinct people only once per customer.

Do coding agents count as seats?

No. Agents, subagents, and worktrees are not additional human seats. Their Cloud activity still consumes measured resources.

How do annual and volume savings combine?

Annual billing includes one month free. Repository and contributor savings combine for up to 20% cumulative savings on standard annual subscriptions.

What happens when we use more resources?

Cloud includes published resource allowances. Usage above an allowance is itemized only for that resource at its disclosed rate, subject to an agreed spending ceiling.

What if we're bigger than the sliders?
