A town full of coworkers.

Your Mac’s home folder becomes your house. Step outside and meet the crew in the streets, shops and workrooms of Siltwick.

Leave a note.

Send a resident a project and a request. Read their reply in your Journal and review proposed edits before they reach your files.

Bring your tools.

Open the built-in browser or terminal when you need it. Choose local models or supported cloud coding agents for your crew.

Take the deep route.

Head to the mines, forge better gear, or test it in the Pearl Ring while your crew gets on with their work.

Install Clack

Open the disk image and drag Clack into Applications.
